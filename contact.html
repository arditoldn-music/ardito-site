Working together

Tell me about your release.

Email me with the subject line "I'd like to work with Ardito" and answers to these questions. It helps me come prepared, whether you want a strategy call, a quote or just a chat.

  1. Your name and artist name.
  2. Your genre, and two artists you sound like.
  3. The track, EP or album name, and the release date (approximate is fine).
  4. A link to the music: SoundCloud, Drive or Dropbox, anything I can stream. Please check it is accessible before you send it.
  5. What is the song about, in your own words?
  6. Have you released music before?
  7. Your approximate following, combined across all platforms.
  8. What do you already have ready? A distributor, a release date, artwork, a press shot, a pre-save or streaming link, a Spotify editorial pitch, a SubmitHub account, any press or playlist adds.
  9. What does success look like for this release? Be specific.
  10. What is your biggest worry about this release?
  11. What have you already tried that hasn't worked?
  12. Your approximate budget for this campaign.
  13. Your Instagram or main social handle.
  14. How did you hear about Ardito?
  15. Anything else I should know?
