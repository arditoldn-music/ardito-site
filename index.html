03 / What I do

From the first pitch to the write-up.

  1. 01

    Radio and blog pitching

    Pitches written for each radio station, blog and outlet, with follow-up through the campaign.

  2. 02

    Playlists

    I'll use free submission sites first. Paid credits only if agreed with the artist and if it's within budget.

  3. 03

    EPK

    I'll create a press kit page of your own, with your bio, music, photos and links.

  4. 04

    Release set-up

    I'll help you sort the admin: Distribution, release date, pre-save link and royalty registration, so radio plays earn money.

  5. 05

    Plan and content

    We'll create a timeline mapped to your release date, and a content plan for before and after. I'll make sure to only include things that fit you and your brand!

  6. 06

    Follow-up and write-up

    I chase unanswered pitches, track what worked, and write it up afterwards.
