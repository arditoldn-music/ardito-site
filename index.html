About

Artist-first.
Always.

Ardito is a release management and artist development agency for early-stage independent artists. We work with artists who are serious about their music and ready to put it out - not artists who already have a team.

Founded by Laura, a Music Business MA graduate (Distinction) from BIMM London with a background spanning live events co-production, touring administration, distribution, and artist management. Laura built Ardito because she kept seeing the same pattern: artists making genuinely good music who had no idea what to do with it once it was finished.

We work with artists under 5,000 followers. That's not a limitation - it's the point. Before the wave is where the work matters most.

Case studies

Two campaigns, same artist, five weeks apart in lead time.

Case 002
July 2026

Omjine — Sweethearting

Seoul-based singer-songwriter. A shift toward Yeah Yeah Yeahs and The xx, built with five weeks of lead time instead of two.

Where Precious One tested what's possible with no runway at all, Sweethearting tested what changes when there's time to actually run a campaign properly. Full pitches, individual follow-ups, an Instagram DM push to radio and curators, and outreach into Omjine's own academic network on top of the usual press list.

The difference showed. Amazing Radio gave it UK and USA airplay and a top 3 Audition Poll place, BBC Introducing's upload was listened to by programming, and five more stations and outlets picked it up, including a reply from a German station written to in German. Zero replies last time, real coverage this time.

Case 001
April 2026

Omjine — Precious One

Seoul-based singer-songwriter. Home recorded, minimal, vocally strong. Somewhere between Cat Power and The xx.

Omjine came to Ardito two weeks before her single was due out with a finished track and no campaign infrastructure behind it. No mailing list, no press contacts, no outreach history. The brief came in without a fee attached — two weeks, one single, build everything from scratch.

What followed was a full release: PRO registration fixed, 62 contacts imported into Mailchimp, 15 personalised pitch emails to blogs and radio stations, Spotify editorial pitch submitted, platform access secured, and two email campaigns written and sent. Precious One is now her most-streamed track.
