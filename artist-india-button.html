Artist

India Button

Northern-born singer-songwriter

Blending atmospheric alt-pop with raw, emotionally charged songwriting, India explores love, loss and the complexities of being human. Her upcoming EP, Baring It All, brings these themes to the forefront, pairing intimate piano-led compositions with expansive production from Ed Allen.

For fans of Freya Ridings, Ashe and London Grammar.

About the release

Baring It All is the title track of India's upcoming EP. The song explores the vulnerability of giving someone every part of yourself, and the strength it takes to reclaim the pieces you've lost along the way. Opening the EP, it sets the scene for a deeply personal journey of reflection, healing and closure.

Produced and mixed by Ed Allen. Demo with Callum Golby. Mastered by Thomas Woodhead.

"Baring It All is a chapter in my life that I wanted to finally face, and be able to close."

India
