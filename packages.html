Your artist page

What happens to it when we finish.

Your page stays live for at least six months after we finish, exactly as it was on our last day, and you keep the PDF. Updates after that are a paid refresh. After six months the page comes down unless we have agreed to keep it up, and anyone opening an old link sees a short note, not an error.

Your words, photos and music stay yours. The template and the site are mine.

I can't promise coverage, plays or playlist adds, and I won't. Radio, blog and playlist pitching is what I do, and I will tell you honestly what I think each release has a chance at. Anything outside a package is quoted separately before I start. Paid platforms, ads and anything you pay a third party for are never included.

Running a label or managing artists? That works differently.
