Hi, I'm Laura

I love a spreadsheet, so you don't have to.

I've worked in live events, touring admin, distribution and artist management, and I did a Music Business MA at BIMM London.

I keep meeting artists who make amazing music but they have no idea what to do with it once it is finished. I love a spreadsheet and a to-do list, so I decided to start helping where I can have the most impact, in the background, supporting their releases.

So far, I only work with artists under 5,000 followers, and I write up every campaign, including planning, what I do, what worked and didn't, etc.

I do this while having a full-time job, so juggling the tasks is a challenge, but a really fun one.

The connections I've made so far are incredibly valuable and already show me that what I'm doing is needed and exactly where I want to be.
