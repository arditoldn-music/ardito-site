ARD 001 · Case study

Omjine · Precious One

A full release campaign built from nothing in two weeks.

Listen on Spotify ↗

The brief

Omjine came to me two weeks before release with a finished track and nothing behind it. No full release plan, no press contacts, no outreach history.
I took it on without a fee to see what's possible with no runway.

What I did

First, the thing nobody had told her: she'd never registered with a collecting society, so any radio play she got would have earned her nothing. I made sure she registered with KOMCA (the South Korean non-profit copyright collective) before anything else.

Then distribution, pitches, platform access, variations of pitches and emails, and 15 pitches to blogs and radio including The Line of Best Fit, BBC Introducing, Soho Radio, Amazing Radio, WFUV and KEXP.

What happened

None of the blog pitches got a reply, which is about what you'd expect with no press history and two weeks ahead of release. The response that did come was from listeners, messaging her to say the song meant something. We made sure to get as many people as we could to presave the track so it would land in their Spotify as soon as it was out. And the contacts now have the track for next time.

What I learned

Press shot needs to come first. I added this as a checklist at onboarding, before anything moves.

Check PRO registration. Almost every early artist is missing it. PRS and PPL in the UK.

15 good pitches beat 50 generic ones.

Blogs and radio need different files and approaches. Private link for blogs, MP3 for radio.
