The brief

Omjine came to me two weeks before release with a finished track and nothing behind it. No mailing list, no press contacts, no outreach history. I took it on without a fee to see what's possible with no runway.

What I did

First, the thing nobody had told her: she'd never registered with a collecting society, so every radio play had earned her nothing. She registered with KOMCA before anything else.

Then distribution, Spotify editorial pitch, platform access, two emails in her voice, and 15 pitches to blogs and radio including The Line of Best Fit, BBC Introducing, Soho Radio, Amazing Radio, WFUV and KEXP.

What happened

None of the pitches got a reply, which is about what you'd expect with no press history and two weeks. The response that came was from listeners, messaging her to say the song meant something. And the contacts now have the track for next time.

What I learned

Press shot first. Now checked at onboarding, before anything moves.

Check PRO registration. Almost every early artist is missing it. PRS and PPL in the UK.

15 good pitches beat 50 generic ones.

Blogs and radio need different files. Private link for blogs, MP3 for radio.
