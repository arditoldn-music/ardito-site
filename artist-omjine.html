Omjine

Singer-songwriter, Seoul

Spotify ↗Instagram ↗

Omjine writes from the perspective most songs skip past. Her latest single, sweethearting, is told from the antagonist's side of someone else's story, quiet on the surface but with something more urgent moving underneath it. Recorded in her home studio, the production stays minimal by choice, letting the vocal and the writing carry the weight.

For fans of Cat Power, The xx and Yeah Yeah Yeahs.
