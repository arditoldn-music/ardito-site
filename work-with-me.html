What I do

The work behind a release, from the first pitch to the write-up.

  • Radio and blog pitching

    Pitches written for each station and blog, sent on a Tuesday morning and followed up one by one.

  • Playlists

    Free submission sites first, then paid credits only where the playlist really fits.

  • EPK

    A press kit page for each artist, hosted here, with bio, music, photos and links.

  • Release set-up

    Distribution, release date, pre-save link and royalty registration, so radio plays earn money.

  • Plan and content

    A timeline mapped to your release date, and a content plan for before and after release day.

  • Follow-up and write-up

    I chase unanswered pitches, track what worked, and write it up afterwards.

  • Release management

    I run the whole campaign with you. Distribution, editorial pitching, blogs, radio, playlists, socials, and the follow-up after release day.
