Your press kit (EPK)

What happens to your press kit when we finish.

Your press kit is the one page I build for you on ardito.co.uk, at ardito.co.uk/epk/your-name, that you send to radio, blogs and venues. After we finish it stays live for at least six months, exactly as it was on our last day, and you keep the PDF. Updates after that are a paid refresh. After six months it comes down unless we have agreed to keep it up, and anyone opening an old link sees a short note, not an error.

Your words, photos and music stay yours. The template and site are mine.

I can't promise coverage, plays or playlist adds, and I won't. Radio, blog and playlist pitching is what I do, and I will tell you honestly what I think each release has a chance at. Anything outside a package is quoted separately before I start. Paid platforms, ads and anything you pay a third party for are never included. Laura x
